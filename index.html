Materials and devices for brain-inspired computing and AI applications.

I'm an Assistant Research Professor in the Device Materials group, Department of Materials Science & Metallurgy, University of Cambridge, and a Researcher at Northeastern University London.

My work sits at the junction of materials development, device fabrication, and multiscale modelling. I am currently interested in oxide-based resistive switching and ferroelectric devices for in-memory and neuromorphic computing hardware. I spend my time across Prof. Judith Driscoll's group at Cambridge and Prof. Bipin Rajendran's group at Northeastern London.

Before Cambridge, I was a postdoctoral research associate at King's College London, and I completed a joint-PhD in Electrical Engineering at IIT Bombay and Materials Science and Engineering at Monash University, following a BS-MS in Physics at IISER Thiruvananthapuram. Across that path, my work has moved from 2D materials and 2D-oxide thin-film-based heterostructures and optoelectronics to oxide-based memristors and the device physics that connects materials choices to circuit-level behaviour.
